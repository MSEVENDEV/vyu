Kinetic.

A dark-first, motion-first design system for Hypt and Vyu. One set of tokens, one motion language, two accents. Everything on this page is live — hover it, press it, switch the product above.

Implemented in WPF (Tokens.xaml · Controls.xaml · Motion.cs) Windows 11 native type & icons Reduced-motion aware
01 · Principles

Five rules everything follows

When a decision isn't covered by a token, these decide.

01

Content is loud, chrome is quiet

Clips and video carry the color. The interface is near-black glass that steps back.

02

Motion explains

Things rise in, presses squash, toggles spring. Nothing animates for more than half a second.

03

One accent per product

Hypt is Blaze → Coral (#FF4500 → #FF6F59). Vyu is Amethyst → Lilac with white. Gradients are spice, not sauce.

04

Native and light

System fonts and icons, no web view, layers that stop drawing the moment they're hidden.

05

Respect the player

Never steal focus from a game, never show up in a clip, honour “animations off”, keep text ≥ 4.5:1.

05 · Motion

Fast start, long soft landing

Five durations, three curves. The Out curve does nearly all the work; Spring is for things you touch.

Out

Exponential ease-out · cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)

Spring

Back ease-out, amplitude 0.35 · cubic-bezier(.34, 1.56, .64, 1)

InOut

Cubic · cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1) — loops & HUD fades

08 · Brand

Two marks, one family

Hypt's mark comes from the brand artwork (design/brand-from-art.ps1 keys it out of its background and builds every icon size). Vyu's mark is drawn in code (design/make-icons.ps1): a purple squircle with a white glyph.

Hypt

An H inside a recording frame — a player leaps off the crossbar and the right stem turns into a camera. Blaze → Coral on charcoal.

Vyu

An eye whose pupil is a play button — watching, simply. Amethyst → Lilac with white.

✓ Do

Keep a quarter of the mark as clear space. Use on dark surfaces. Minimum 16 px.

✕ Don't

Recolor the glyph, swap the two gradients, stretch the squircle or add outlines.

Names

Hypt reads “hyped”. Vyu reads “view”. Always one word, capital first letter.
